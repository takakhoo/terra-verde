What's Inside

Organic Greens & Vegetable Blend: Spirulina, Chlorella (cracked cell wall), Wheatgrass, Barley Grass, Alfalfa, Kale, Spinach, Broccoli, Moringa, Parsley, Nettle Leaf.

Antioxidant Fruit Blend: Beetroot, Acai, Blueberry, Pomegranate, Acerola Cherry.

Fiber & Prebiotic Blend: Inulin (chicory root), Apple Pectin, Flaxseed.

Adaptogen & Herb Blend: Maca Root, Rhodiola Rosea, Ginger, Turmeric, Astragalus.

Digestive Enzyme & Live Cultures Blends: Amylase, Protease, Lipase, Cellulase, Bromelain; L. acidophilus, B. bifidum, L. rhamnosus.

How to use

Mix 1 scoop (10 g) into 8-12 oz of cold water, juice or a smoothie once daily. Shake or stir until dissolved. Best enjoyed in the morning.
